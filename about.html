About American Nails.

A small nail, hair and beauty salon at 20 Park Street — open and busy on the same corner of the town centre since 2002.

A nail bar by name and a proper hair-and-beauty salon by trade. We've worked from this same shop on Park Street since 2002 — long enough to know most of our customers by their drink order, never long enough to think we've seen it all.

We don't push add-ons. We don't sell you a treatment plan. If you've come in for a gel manicure and you've got half an hour, that's a gel manicure for half an hour — polished, checked under the lamp and out the door. If you've come in for a full colour and you want the chair for the afternoon, that's yours too.

The work is unfussy and careful. Cuticles done by hand. Acrylics filed properly. Blow-dries actually styled rather than just dried. Wax that's warm, not hot. Threading by someone who's been doing it for longer than most of our customers have been driving.

Most people come in as walk-ins. For colour, occasion hair, or anything that needs longer in the chair, please ring ahead — we'll fit you in. The kettle is always on; you're welcome any time we're open.
