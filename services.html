Services

What we do,
by the chair.

Nails, hair, beauty — three trades on one shop floor at 20 Park Street. Prices are posted at the front of the salon and confirmed before we start. Walk in or call to check availability.

Nails

Walk-ins welcome · most sets 30 – 75 minutes

  • Gel manicure

    Long-wear glossy finish. Shape, cuticle work, base, two coats and top — checked under the lamp. Comes off cleanly when you want a fresh set.

  • Acrylic full set

    Tips applied, shaped to your liking — square, oval, almond, coffin or stiletto — and finished in colour, French or chrome. Infills every two to three weeks.

  • Dip powder

    Strong, thin finish that doesn't lift. Good for natural-length nails that chip easily or anyone who's hard on their hands.

  • Builder gel overlay

    A protective gel over the natural nail — adds strength without extensions. The honest middle ground for growing nails out.

  • Pedicure

    Soak, scrub, hard-skin work, cuticle, shape, polish — gel or regular. A proper half-hour off your feet.

  • Nail art & chrome

    From a single accent to a full hand-painted set. Chrome powders, French tips, ombré, gems. Bring a photo if you've got one in mind.

  • Men's manicure & pedicure

    Quiet, no-polish finish for hands or feet — cuticle work, shape, buff. Half-hour each, or both together while you read the paper.

Hair

By appointment for colour · walk-in for cuts

  • Cut & blow-dry

    Consult, wash, cut and blow-dry — actually styled rather than just dried. Men's and children's cuts too.

  • Blow-dry & style

    For an occasion, a wedding, or just a Friday night. Smooth, curled or set — whichever you came in for.

  • Full head colour

    Single-process colour, root-to-tip. Please ring ahead so we can set the chair aside.

  • Highlights, foils & babylights

    Partial or full head. Hand-painted balayage on request. Allow two to three hours.

  • Toner & gloss

    For brassy blondes or faded colour. About forty minutes and you walk out a different shade.

  • Perm & chemical straightening

    Patch test required forty-eight hours before. Please ring to book the consultation first.

  • Bridal & occasion

    Hair, nails and brows together if you'd like. Trials at quiet times in the week. Book well ahead for Saturdays in spring and summer.

Beauty

In the back room · ten minutes to an hour

  • Eyebrow threading

    Done in about ten minutes by someone who's been doing it for longer than most. Clean lines, no wax burns.

  • Upper lip / chin threading

    Quick. Quiet. In and out without a fuss.

  • Brow shape & tint

    Shape first, then a vegetable tint. Patch test forty-eight hours before for first-timers.

  • Lash lift

    Your own lashes, curled up and tinted. Takes about forty-five minutes. Lasts six to eight weeks.

  • Classic lash extensions

    One extension per lash. Light, natural-looking — not heavy. Infills every two to three weeks.

  • Waxing

    Half-leg, full-leg, underarm, arm, lip, chin. Warm wax, clean strips.

  • Facials

    Cleanse, exfoliate, mask, moisturise. A proper rest with the lights low.
