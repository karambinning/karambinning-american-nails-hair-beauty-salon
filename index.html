About the salon

A quiet hour
on Park Street.

American Nails is a small hair, nails and beauty salon in the centre of Luton. The kind of place you nip into on a Saturday morning for a fresh set, or settle into on a Thursday evening for colour, conversation and a proper finish.

Our work is unfussy and careful. We take time over a cuticle the way other people take time over a haircut. Whether you want a clean, polished set for the week ahead or something bigger for an occasion, the chair is yours.

Walk-ins are welcome whenever the door is open. For colour and longer appointments, drop in during the week to book.
