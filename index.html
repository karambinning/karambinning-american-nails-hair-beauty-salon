American Nails has been a small nails, hair and beauty salon in the centre of Luton since 2002. The kind of place you nip into on a Saturday morning for a fresh set, or settle into on a Thursday evening for colour, conversation and a proper finish.

Our work is unfussy and careful. We take time over a cuticle the way other people take time over a haircut — whether you want a clean polished set for the week ahead, a sharp acrylic full-set for an occasion, or just a half-hour off your feet.

Walk-ins are welcome whenever the door is open. For colour and longer appointments, please ring ahead or drop in during the week to book.
