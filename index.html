About the salon

A small Luton salon for the regulars and the in-betweens.

Tucked onto Park Street, between the shops and the bus stops, we are a hair-and-nails room rather than a flashy chain.

The space is small on purpose. One conversation at a time, one set of hands at a time, and a proper unhurried finish on whatever you came in for — a tidy gel, a fresh cut, brows that sit straight again.

Most of our work comes from people who live and work within ten minutes of the front door. Lunch-break manicures. Friday-afternoon colour. The mum who pops in with the toddler. The wedding-week panic. We try to make all of those feel the same: looked-after, not rushed.

If you are new to us, the easiest thing is to walk in. We will tell you honestly what we can do today and what would be better booked for another day.

Small room. Steady hands. A long list of regulars, and always room for one more. The house style
